# Music is only one layer

For an effect tutorial, record a cue sheet with source item/timecode, visual event, BGM, transient SFX, continuous sound, native resource ID, source trim, timeline frames, gain in dB and fade durations. Keep absent facts unknown. Text that says “add music” is not proof there are no additional accents.

Inspect continuous motion and closely spaced frames through the change. Compare the audio waveform/transients around event times. If local source effects exist, correlation can support identity and approximate timing; mixtures and compression prevent calling it exact stem recovery. Preserve the chosen BGM start unless changing it is authorized or required and evidenced.

Use independent tracks. Equal gain values do not imply equal loudness: compare source levels, then balance accents against the music. Use true-peak and loudness to find gross errors, not to claim taste or timing sounds right. A continuous mechanical loop and a short attack can require different gain and fade settings. Choose both against the current mix.

Export natively when required. Check actual decoded audio, channels/duration, true peak, fades and synchronization against frame events. Separately record **program measurement**, **actual frame review**, **actual listening**, and **user feedback**. If audition is unavailable, deliver the preview and say listening is pending; do not activate an audio-quality recipe. The operation of setting a measured gain can still be validated independently.
